Party panel

A throwaway check of the battle HUD's draft visual direction (see docs/battle.md): two rows per member, HP and TP bars at half width with the numbers inside, a status row, an ATB ring around the portrait, and the green to black HP ramp. Drag the sliders, or press Run to watch it fill and drain like a fight. Characters are placeholder shapes.

The battle screen is a fixed 16:9 game view that scales to fit, so text scale is relative to the screen, as it would be in the game. The game isn't designed for phones or touch: only the controls around the screen rearrange on a small window.

The second strip is the same ramp with the color removed. It gets steadily darker from full to zero, so "darker is worse" holds even for a player who can't tell green from red. That's why the middle stop is amber, not bright yellow: bright yellow is lighter than green and would break the order. The color-vision setting above applies here too.

What to judge

  • 2× text with Max HP 9999. Does "9999/9999" still fit inside a half-width bar, and does four members' worth of panel leave the field visible?
  • Ready vs acting. Run it and watch: a thick ring means ready, the lit row means acting. Can you tell them apart when two members are ready at once?
  • Numbers over the fill edge. Drag HP slowly through the middle. The digits change ink where the fill ends; check they stay readable at every value.
  • Backdrops and color vision. Snow is the hardest case for the panel edge. Deuteranopia is the hardest for the ramp.
  • The status row. It's always there, even empty. Is the extra height worth a layout that never jumps?
  • The camera angle. "Ours" is 15° down at 7 units. Exploration is close to the first-person view: the party stands in front of the enemies and hides them. Chrono Trigger looks further down so both sides spread up the screen. With "Upright" billboards (the engine today), looking down squashes them; "Tilted to the camera" keeps them full height.
  • The idle pan. Press "Play idle pan", or leave a menu open for 8 seconds. The camera swings round and up while the cards keep their facing: they go paper-thin, show as lines from straight above, then flip back to face you. Click anything to cut it short. "Turn" and "Freeze billboards" let you stop at any point of it.
  • Moving in combat. Press Run: attackers run to their target, hit, and run back across the ground. Watch the menu: it only moves if someone ends up under it.
  • Nobody gets covered. Any character the HUD covers gets a dashed outline, and "Show the open field" draws where characters should stay. Try 2× text with four members: the panel grows, the open field shrinks, and the battle area would have to shrink with it.
  • The menu moves only when it has to. It starts beside the party panel, in the HUD band and off the field. Drag the acting character (the one with the ring) under it and let go: the menu moves once, to the next free slot. It doesn't follow anyone, so it never jitters while characters move.
  • Opposites cancel. Give Triangle "Attack down": it cancels the Attack up, and both icons go. Poison and regen are the exception. Square has both: press Run and watch its HP drop and recover on separate ticks.
