Things to try

  1. Chop a triangle from a different lane. With a 14-unit depth tolerance you have to line up first. That's Final Fight's whole feel, and its main frustration. Slide the tolerance until it stops being annoying.
  2. Wait for a rhombus to leap, then chop. It's above the chop, so you miss. Sweep instead: slower to come out and costs more POW, but it catches the rhombus in the air and hits across lanes.
  3. Watch the shadows while jumping. In a depth lane the shadow is the only way to tell where you'll land.
  4. Swing until POW runs out. Sweeps drain it faster. Does the choice between a cheap short chop and an expensive wide sweep carry a fight?
  5. Heal mid-fight with I. The fight slows behind the menu instead of stopping. Open it as a triangle winds up and see if you can heal before the lunge lands. Slide the menu speed: 0% is a pause (FF, Odin Sphere), 100% is real time.
  6. Turn starting items and drops down to zero-ish. How few heals does a ring need before blocking and POW start to matter?
  7. Stand in a triangle's way on purpose. Every few hits you survive raise CON until the area cap. Then hit Reset ring: the enemies come back, the CON doesn't. Does it feel like a reward for getting hit, or a consolation?
  8. Grow STR, then go back. Weapon levels raise STR for the boulder, which hides a Full Circle. Flip the checks to d20 and compare.
